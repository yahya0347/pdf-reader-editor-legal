Privacy Policy

App: PDF Reader & Editor (Android) · Package: com.muhammadyahyaaslam.pdfreadereditor
Effective date: 22 October 2026 · Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application PDF Reader & Editor (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by Muhammad Yahya Aslam, an individual developer based in Pakistan ("we", "us", "the developer").

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Short summary

  • Your PDF files are opened and edited on your device. We do not upload, copy, read or store your documents on any server.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • The App shows advertisements. Our advertising partner may collect limited device and usage data to serve ads.
  • Google ML Kit, which powers translation, sends Google diagnostics and usage data. It never sends your documents or their text.

2. Your documents stay on your device

All PDF viewing, editing, signing, merging, splitting, compressing and converting is performed locally on your device. The App does not transmit the content of your documents to us, and we have no access to the files you open.

The App keeps a "Recent files" list so you can quickly reopen documents. This list is stored only in the App's local storage on your device, and the App never sends it to us. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may copy this list, your theme setting and the preference files kept on your device by the Google components inside the App to your own Google account backup, so they come back when you set up a new phone. Those preference files belong to Google's advertising, ad consent and web view components and can include ad settings, advertising-related identifiers, your ad consent choice and a record of the websites the ad view has opened. The App does not read them, and we never receive them. You can clear the list from inside the App, and it is permanently removed when you uninstall the App.

Your saved signature. If you save a signature, the App stores the pen strokes you drew on your device only, encrypted with a key that never leaves the phone. It is never uploaded and never included in Android backups. You can remove it at any time in Settings → Delete saved signature.

3. Information we collect

We, as the developer, do not collect any personal information from you directly. We operate no servers and no user accounts.

However, third-party services integrated into the App may automatically collect certain information. These are described in Section 5.

4. Device permissions and why they are used

  • Files and documents — the App requests no storage permission. You open and save PDFs through Android's system file picker, so the App only accesses the files and folders you explicitly select. It does not scan or browse your storage in the background.
  • Internet access — required to display advertisements, to download translation languages you agree to download, and used by Google ML Kit to send its diagnostics and usage data (see Section 5).
  • Advertising ID — used by our advertising partner to show ads. You can reset or delete this ID at any time in your Android settings.
  • Camera — not requested by the App. Scanning uses Google's document scanner, which runs its own camera screen (see Section 5).

None of these permissions can be switched off individually in your device settings: Android grants them when the App is installed. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android settings, and uninstalling the App removes all of them.

5. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, device type, operating system version, and interactions with ads. This is used to deliver and measure advertising.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can limit personalised advertising on your device through Settings → Google → Ads, or by resetting your advertising ID.

Ad consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and your choice is respected. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Google Play Services and the document scanner

The App uses Google Play Services for standard functions such as app distribution and advertising delivery. Tools → Scan opens Google's document scanner, part of Google Play services. The scanner runs its own camera screen, processes pages on your device and hands the finished PDF back to the App, which saves it where you choose. Google Play services may download the scanner itself the first time you use it. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Translation (on your device)

Tools → Translate translates a PDF's text on your device using Google ML Kit. Your document text is not sent to Google or to us. To translate, the phone needs a language file for each language (English is built in); before downloading one, the App asks you. The download comes from Google's servers, which, like any download, see your IP address. After that, translation works offline.

ML Kit diagnostics and usage data. Google ML Kit, which provides translation and language detection, sends Google information for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information, app information, identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input and output size, feature version, event type and error codes, together with the source and target languages you chose and the languages it identified. For translation, ML Kit also uses Firebase installations and Firebase Remote Config, which create an installation identifier for this copy of the App. The text you translate is not part of this data. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and that ML Kit does not transfer it to third parties. Source: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

Delete your data

Published by: Muhammad Yahya Aslam · m.yahyaaslamofficial@gmail.com

PDF Reader & Editor has no accounts and no server. Your documents and everything the app stores stay on your phone, so you delete them on your phone. There is nothing held by us to request deletion of.

Delete part of your data

To deleteDo this
One file from RecentHome → tap the trash icon on that file
All of RecentSettings → Clear recent files
Your saved signatureSettings → Delete saved signature
A folder you addedFiles → tap the trash icon on that folder (the app also gives its permission back to Android)

Removing a file from Recent does not delete the file itself from your phone — it only removes it from the app's list.

Delete everything

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → PDF Reader & Editor → Storage → Clear storage. This removes the Recent list, settings, your signature, added folders and any translation languages you downloaded.

Android backup

If Android backup is on, your Recent list and theme may be in your own Google account backup. To remove them, turn off backup for the app or delete the backup in Android Settings → Google → Backup. Your signature is never included in backups.

Advertising data

Advertising data is collected by Google AdMob, not by us. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and manage Google's data at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy
