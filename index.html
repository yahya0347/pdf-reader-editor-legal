Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026 App: PDF Reader & Editor (Android) Published by: Muhammad Yahya Aslam Contact: m.yahyaaslamofficial@gmail.com

This policy explains what PDF Reader & Editor does with your information. It is written to be read, not to be impressive. Where something leaves your phone, we say so.

The short version

  • Your documents stay on your phone. PDFs are opened, edited, merged, split, compressed, scanned and translated on the device. We have no server, so we never receive your documents.
  • The app can only see files and folders you choose through Android's file picker. It has no permission to browse your storage.
  • Translation runs on your phone. The first time you translate into a language, the app asks before downloading that language (about 30 MB) from Google. Your document text is not sent anywhere.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects your device's Advertising ID, your IP address (which can show your general location), how you interact with the app and its ads, and basic performance diagnostics — for ads, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

1. Who we are

PDF Reader & Editor is published by Muhammad Yahya Aslam. You can reach us at m.yahyaaslamofficial@gmail.com.

2. We do not run a server

The app has no backend, no user accounts and no cloud storage. Our own code does not upload your documents or anything about them. Three pieces of software bundled in the app do talk to Google, and they are described below:

  • the Google AdMob advertising SDK (section 5),
  • the Google document scanner, provided by Google Play services (section 6), and
  • ML Kit translation, which downloads language files from Google (section 7).

We say this plainly because "the app sends no data" would be misleading. The app itself sends none of your documents. Those Google components connect to Google as described.

3. Your documents

  • Files are opened through Android's Storage Access Framework — the system file picker. The app sees only the files and folders you pick, and keeps access to them only so they can be reopened from Recent or the Files tab.
  • Edits are saved as a new copy in a place you choose. The original file is never changed.
  • Page images used while you read or edit are kept in the app's temporary storage and deleted automatically.
  • Document contents are never written to logs.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is funded by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which connects to Google's servers and automatically collects:

  • your device's Advertising ID and app set ID — resettable identifiers Android provides for advertising
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region — never your precise location; the app has no location permission)
  • interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, ad views and taps
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is collected by Google, not by us, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — and described in Google's disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

  • We do not send your documents, file names, text or signature to AdMob or to any advertiser.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ads appear in a banner on the Home, Files, Tools and Settings tabs and on the tool screens (Merge, Split, Image to PDF, Compress, Translate). Ads never appear while you read, edit, sign, scan or print a document.

6. Scanning

Tools → Scan opens Google's document scanner, part of Google Play services. The scanner runs its own camera screen: the app itself has no camera permission. The scanner processes pages on your phone and hands the finished PDF back to the app, which saves it where you choose. Google Play services may download the scanner itself the first time you use it. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Translation

Tools → Translate reads the text of a PDF and translates it on your phone using Google ML Kit. Your document text is not sent to Google or to us.

To translate, the phone needs a language file for each language (English is built in). Before downloading one, the app asks you. The download comes from Google's servers, which, like any download, see your IP address. After that, translation works offline. Translations are machine-generated and can contain mistakes.

8. Printing and sharing

When you print, the PDF goes to the print service you choose in Android's print screen. When you share or save a file, it goes where you choose. If you pick a cloud printer or an online service, that service handles the file under its own policy.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and because we operate no server we hold no such information.

11. Deleting your data

  • Settings → Clear recent files removes the whole Recent list; the trash icon on a row removes one file.
  • Settings → Delete saved signature removes your signature.
  • Files tab → trash icon forgets a folder and gives its permission back to Android.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, including downloaded translation languages.

Because your documents never reach us, there is nothing for you to ask us to delete. See the data deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

Delete your data

Published by: Muhammad Yahya Aslam · m.yahyaaslamofficial@gmail.com

PDF Reader & Editor has no accounts and no server. Your documents and everything the app stores stay on your phone, so you delete them on your phone. There is nothing held by us to request deletion of.

Delete part of your data

To deleteDo this
One file from RecentHome → tap the trash icon on that file
All of RecentSettings → Clear recent files
Your saved signatureSettings → Delete saved signature
A folder you addedFiles → tap the trash icon on that folder (the app also gives its permission back to Android)

Removing a file from Recent does not delete the file itself from your phone — it only removes it from the app's list.

Delete everything

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → PDF Reader & Editor → Storage → Clear storage. This removes the Recent list, settings, your signature, added folders and any translation languages you downloaded.

Android backup

If Android backup is on, your Recent list and theme may be in your own Google account backup. To remove them, turn off backup for the app or delete the backup in Android Settings → Google → Backup. Your signature is never included in backups.

Advertising data

Advertising data is collected by Google AdMob, not by us. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and manage Google's data at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy
